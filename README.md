## 🪪 Who's running this shop

I'm Sonu — final-year BCA student who somehow ended up building a civic-sanitation
AI platform, an encrypted offline chat app, an AI teacher, and a couple of games,
under one banner: **Silver Soul Studios**. (Yes, that's a direct translation of
"Gintama." No, that's not a coincidence.)

I don't specialize in one stack because the problems I find interesting don't
specialize either — sanitation, security, agriculture, education, emergencies,
and the occasional game are all "jobs," and a Yorozuya takes the job, not just
the jobs that match its resume.

Off-repo: bleeds red and gold for RCB, hoards diecast cars the way most people
hoard tech debt, and still unironically quotes Gintama at 2 AM. On-repo: ships
first, hardens second — some people call that "vibe coding," I call it agile
with the meetings removed.

## ⚔️ The Yorozuya Principle

> *If the client's problem is real, the stack is negotiable.*

A few things that actually shape how I build, not just what I say I believe:

- **Ship, then survive contact.** MeshWhisper went through two full rounds of
  security auditing after the first working demo — the working demo was the
  easy part.
- **Audits aren't optional, they're the job.** Fixed critical bugs in ACK
  dedup, unauthenticated ACK packets, and panic-wipe logic — none of which
  show up until someone actually tries to break it.
- **"Done" is a checkpoint, not an exit.** InfraWatch Nexus won Best Paper at
  ICAHTE-2026 and is still on the open-cases list, because winning a paper
  award and having a production-hardened system are different bars.
